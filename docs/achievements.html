Achievements & Impact

Records, titles, and influence on the game

Individual Honors

  • Ballon d’Or: 8 wins (a record) — 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021, 2023
  • FIFA World Player of the Year / The Best FIFA Men’s Player: multiple wins
  • UEFA Men’s Player of the Year: multiple wins
  • European Golden Shoe: 6 wins (a record), awarded to Europe’s top league scorer each season
  • FIFA World Cup Golden Ball: 2014 and 2022

Team Honors

With FC Barcelona

  • La Liga titles: 10
  • Copa del Rey titles: 7
  • UEFA Champions League titles: 4
  • FIFA Club World Cup titles: 3

With Paris Saint-Germain

  • Ligue 1 titles: 2

With Argentina

  • FIFA World Cup: 2022
  • Copa América: 2021, 2024
  • CONMEBOL–UEFA Cup of Champions (Finalissima): 2022

Records

  • All-time leading goalscorer for FC Barcelona
  • All-time leading goalscorer for the Argentina national team
  • Most goals scored in a calendar year (91, in 2012)
  • Most Ballon d’Or awards won by any player
  • Most European Golden Shoe awards won by any player
  • One of very few players to win a World Cup, multiple Champions Leagues, and multiple Ballon d’Or awards across a career

Impact on the Sport

Messi’s influence extends well beyond individual statistics:

  • Redefining the forward position — his combination of dribbling, playmaking, and finishing influenced a generation of attacking players and coaching philosophies built around false-nine and inverted-winger roles.
  • Global growth of soccer in the United States — his 2023 move to Inter Miami CF drove record ticket sales, broadcast viewership, and merchandise demand, contributing meaningfully to the sport’s growing popularity in North America ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
  • A model of longevity and consistency — sustaining elite performance across nearly two decades at the highest level of European football and international competition.
  • Cultural icon — one of the most-followed athletes in the world on social media, and a symbol of Argentine sporting pride, especially following the 2022 World Cup triumph.
In his own words

“Success is not a coincidence… It is hard work, perseverance, learning, studying, sacrifice, and most of all, love for what you are doing.”
