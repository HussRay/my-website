Background & Career

From Rosario to global stardom

Early Life

Lionel Andrés Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina, the third of four children. He began playing football as a young child with his local club, Grandoli, coached by his father, Jorge Messi.

At age 11, Messi was diagnosed with a growth-hormone deficiency, a condition that required expensive treatment his family struggled to afford. This medical challenge became a turning point in his career: FC Barcelona, impressed by his talent, offered to cover the cost of his treatment if his family relocated to Spain — an offer his family accepted in 2000.

Rise Through La Masia

Messi joined FC Barcelona’s famed youth academy, La Masia, where he progressed rapidly through the club’s youth ranks. He made his first-team debut for Barcelona in October 2004 at age 17, quickly establishing himself as an integral part of the squad.

Club Career

FC Barcelona (2004–2021) Messi spent the majority of his career at Barcelona, becoming the club’s all-time leading scorer and helping the team win numerous domestic and international trophies, including multiple UEFA Champions League titles. He formed a devastating attacking partnership with players like Xavi, Andrés Iniesta, and later Luis Suárez and Neymar as part of the celebrated “MSN” trio.

Paris Saint-Germain (2021–2023) In August 2021, Messi moved to Paris Saint-Germain, where he continued to add domestic silverware to his trophy cabinet while adapting to a new league and system.

Inter Miami CF (2023–present) In 2023, Messi joined Inter Miami CF in Major League Soccer, a move that generated significant global attention for American soccer and helped drive record interest, attendance, and merchandise sales for the league.

International Career

Messi made his debut for the Argentina national team in 2005 and has gone on to become the country’s most-capped player and all-time leading goalscorer. After several near-misses in major finals, he captained Argentina to victory at the 2021 Copa América and the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar — a tournament widely considered the crowning achievement of his career, delivered on a dramatic penalty-shootout final against France.
